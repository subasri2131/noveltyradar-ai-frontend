About NoveltyRadar AI

NoveltyRadar AI is an evidence-based, explainable research and student project evaluation platform designed to help students and researchers assess the novelty and feasibility of their project ideas.

Mission

Our mission is to provide transparent, AI-powered project evaluation that helps students make informed decisions about their research and project directions.

How It Works

  • Analyze project claims and extract key components
  • Search relevant research papers from academic databases
  • Perform claim-wise gap analysis against existing literature
  • Calculate novelty and feasibility scores with explainable reasoning
  • Provide improved project directions and recommendations

Technology

NoveltyRadar AI uses advanced NLP techniques, semantic search, and machine learning to provide comprehensive project evaluation. The platform integrates with Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex for academic paper retrieval.
